01 Invoice in depth Matching section · replaces the flat lens

The invoice lies back like a sheet on a desk, and a scan runs down it. Verified lines settle flat with their tick. Held lines rise off the page toward you, turn amber and cast a shadow, so what the check found stands out at a glance.

Cost: CSS 3D on the existing rows. No library. Scroll to play.

02 Starlink globe Installation section · alongside or instead of the satellite card

A dot-matrix Earth turns to the UK. A gate pings, and its check-ins climb to a satellite and come down to UK servers. As satellites pass, the link hands over from one to the next. Orbits are drawn at their real height, 550 km; the passes are sped up. Drag the globe to spin it.

Cost: the COBE globe, about 12 KB with its WebGL helper, bundled with the site. Pauses off screen; phones get a smaller globe; reduced motion gets the finished frame.

03 Hero depth Hero · the film's last frame, where the card locks on

The film, the viewfinder corners, the words and the card sit on separate planes and shift with the pointer, so the card reads as glass floating in front of the gate. The leader stays tied to the terminal, and the glass catches the light as it tilts.

Cost: a few lines of script, transform only. Mouse and trackpad only; phones keep the still composition.
