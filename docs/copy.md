## 3. Proof — how it works
- Eyebrow: How it works
- H2: **From gate scan to approved payment.**
- 01 Worker verified — Identity confirmed at a fixed device on site entry: face or fingerprint, with GPS location. No cards, PINs or proxy sign-ins.
- 02 Hours captured — In and out times recorded per worker, per day. Works offline and syncs when the connection returns.
- 03 Invoice checked — Every claimed line is compared against the verified attendance record.
- 04 Exceptions raised — Anything without a matching record is flagged before payroll, grouped by type.
- 05 Payment released — Only verified labour is paid. Every payment carries its evidence.
- Aside: Sits alongside your existing payroll and timekeeping. Export, reconcile, keep what works.

## 4. The leaks
- Eyebrow: Where money goes
- H2: **Three leaks. One record closes them.**
- Ghost workers — Names on the invoice that never passed the gate: fake, absent or duplicated. *Caught by:* no scan on the billed day.
- Inflated hours — Claims that outrun the shift: extended hours, padded overtime, wrong start times. *Caught by:* clock-out records that contradict the claim.
- Proxy clocking — One person signing in for another: buddy clocking, shared cards, unsupervised check-ins. *Caught by:* two scans for one identity at the same moment.

## 8. FAQ
- H2: **Questions finance teams ask first.**
- How is attendance evidence collected? — A fixed device at site entry records each arrival and departure against a registered biometric profile, face or fingerprint. No paper registers, swipe cards or proxy sign-ins.
- Does it replace our payroll or timekeeping system? — No. It sits alongside them as a verification layer. Attendance data exports and reconciles with what you already run.
- How is biometric data stored? — Templates are encrypted and held on UK-based servers, never shared with third parties, and deleted on request or at contract end. Workers give consent at onboarding and a Data Processing Agreement is available.
- How quickly can we deploy? — Most sites go live within about two weeks. Hardware arrives pre-configured and workers enrol on arrival.
- What if a subcontractor refuses to use it? — Compliance is set at contract level, usually as a condition of site access. A worker who does not check in is not counted as present, and is not invoiced for.
- Do workers need a smartphone or an app? — No. Check-in happens at a fixed device. Workers install nothing.
