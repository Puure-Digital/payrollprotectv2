01The process

Five Steps to a Live Gate.

  1. 01

    Delivered Pre-Configured

    The gate terminal arrives set up for your site. There is nothing to configure.

  2. 02

    Mounted at Site Entry

    The terminal fixes at the gate, where every worker passes. It is ruggedised for outdoor conditions.

  3. 03

    Connected by Starlink

    The dish goes where it has a clear view of the sky. Once it is up, the gate is online, with no site Wi-Fi, phone line or mobile signal needed.

  4. 04

    Workers Enrol on Arrival

    Each worker registers a face or fingerprint at the terminal the first time they arrive. They install nothing and need no smartphone.

  5. 05

    Live on Site

    Every check-in is matched line by line against the invoice. Most sites are live within two weeks.
