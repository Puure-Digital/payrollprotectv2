05How it works

From gate scan to approved payment.

  1. 01

    Worker verified

    Identity confirmed at a fixed device on site entry: face or fingerprint, with GPS location. No cards, PINs or proxy sign-ins.

  2. 02

    Hours captured

    In and out times recorded per worker, per day. Works offline and syncs when the connection returns.

  3. 03

    Invoice checked

    Every claimed line is compared against the verified attendance record.

  4. 04

    Exceptions raised

    Anything without a matching record is flagged before payroll, grouped by type.

  5. 05

    Payment released

    Only verified labour is paid. Every payment carries its evidence.

Sits alongside your existing payroll and timekeeping. Export, reconcile, keep what works.

06Installation

Any gate. Online by satellite.

Every terminal connects over Starlink satellite internet, so the gate doesn’t depend on site Wi-Fi or a mobile signal.

Connected by

  1. 01
    Mounted at the gate

    Arrives pre-configured, ready to fix at site entry.

  2. 02
    Online by Starlink

    The dish gives the gate its own connection, independent of the site office.

  3. 03
    Every scan, sent

    Each check-in goes straight to UK-based servers, ready to match against the invoice.

  4. 04
    Offline, still recording

    If the link drops, scans queue at the gate and sync when it returns.
